About MYA Silk Mills

Three Generations of Silk Excellence

Established in 1985, our enterprise manufactures, imports, and supplies premium metallic yarns, zari yarns, and dori yarns under the brand name Coloured Thread.

We cater specifically to the embroidery industry across the United States and have earned a reputation as one of the top metallic yarn manufacturers in the country through decades of dedication to quality and innovation.

Premium Quality

We use only the finest materials and advanced manufacturing techniques to ensure superior quality in every thread.

Modern Facilities

Our plants feature world-class lacquering machines, high-speed slitters, and micro-slitters for precision manufacturing.

Family Legacy

Three generations of expertise and dedication to the silk and embroidery industry since our establishment.
